CCSB Biweekly Seminar Series

CCSB Biweekly Seminar Series promotes interdisciplinary research across PVAMU campus and beyond, hosted by the CRI Center for Computational Systems Biology. We alternate between CCSB researchers and external speakers. We use a hybrid format, between in-person meet and virtual meet (Zoom) based on speaker's preference. When we meet in person, we will meet in CCSB Conference Room (ELEN 231). Dr. Seungchan Kim is the organizer for the seminar series. Please contact him if you have any suggestions, including speakers.
