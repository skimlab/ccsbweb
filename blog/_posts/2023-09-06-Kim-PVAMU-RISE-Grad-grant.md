[Dr. Kim](/team/seungchan-kim) was recently awarded a PVAMU RISE Graduate grant (internal) to support a Graduate Research Assistant (GRA).  


<br/>

**Project Title:** Development of Computational Tool to Investigate Cell signaling utilizing Spatial Transcriptomic Data

**Principal Investigator:** Seungchan Kim, Ph.D.

**Project Summary:**

This proposal aims to develop a computational tool to analyze spatial transcriptomic data, investigating cell-cell signaling by integrating spatial information and gene expression data. The tool will utilize existing methods for cell-type classification and prioritize interactions between co-localized cells using a curated database of ligand-receptor interactions. The research has broad implications for understanding tumor-microenvironment interactions and potential drug targets for various diseases.
