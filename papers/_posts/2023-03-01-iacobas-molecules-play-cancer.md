# Abstract

This editorial introduces the Special Issue on Molecules at Play in Cancer, highlighting recent breakthroughs in identifying the genomic, transcriptomic, and molecular mechanisms governing oncogenesis, tumor progression, and metastasis. We emphasize the necessity of systems biology and network-level analyses to overcome the limitations of single-gene biomarker paradigms and formulate targeted, individualized cancer therapeutics.
